# sdvplotR: Sports Team Logo Plots in 'ggplot2', 'gt' and 'reactable'

A unified set of functions to visualize sports data across the 28
leagues of the SportsDataverse team index, including the National
Football League, National Basketball Association, Women's National
Basketball Association, Major League Baseball, National Hockey League,
college football and basketball, and soccer, in 'ggplot2' plots, 'gt'
tables and 'reactable' tables, and in interactive 'plotly', 'ggiraph'
and 'leaflet' charts and maps. Team logos, wordmarks and player
headshots can be drawn as plot layers, as axis text and inside table
cells, in the design a team used in a given season, and team color
scales map team identifiers to their official colors. Playing surfaces
from 'sportyR' can be painted in a team's colors. Team identifiers from
many data providers, including the codes of relocated franchises, are
resolved to one canonical team id per league through the team index and
logo manifest of the SportsDataverse logo archive
<https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sdv-assets>, read at run time with a
copy bundled in the package. Built on 'ggpath' following the conventions
of 'nflplotR' and designed to work with the companion SportsDataverse
data packages. Also includes the 'gtUtils' table themes, color legends,
cut lines, significance notation, outlier flags and cropping and saving
helpers for 'gt' tables.
