# sdvplotR: Sports Team Logo Plots in 'ggplot2', 'gt' and 'reactable'

A unified set of functions to visualize sports data across the National
Football League, National Basketball Association, Women's National
Basketball Association, Major League Baseball, National Hockey League,
and college football and basketball in 'ggplot2' plots, 'gt' tables and
'reactable' tables. Team logos, wordmarks and player headshots can be
drawn as plot layers, as axis text and inside table cells, and team
color scales map team identifiers to their official colors. Team
abbreviations used by different data providers, including historical
abbreviations of relocated franchises, are cleaned to one canonical key
per sport. Built on 'ggpath' following the conventions of 'nflplotR' and
designed to work with the companion 'SportsDataverse' data packages.
