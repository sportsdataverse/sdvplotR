### Cookbooks

Task-oriented, cross-league recipes: logos and headshots in ggplot2,
team colors and themes, interactive charts, playing surfaces and shot
charts, and reactable tables.

- [Logos, wordmarks and headshots in
  ggplot2](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-ggplot-logos.md):

  Twelve ggplot2 recipes: logos as points, at bar and line ends, on axes
  and beside titles, in tier lists, plus sizing, overlap, past seasons’
  marks, any image, headshots and social-size exports.

- [Team colors and
  themes](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-colors-themes.md):

  Ten recipes for team colors: a league’s palette, colors per row, team
  fill and outline scales, clashing colors, readable text on team fills,
  named palettes, conference colors, dark backgrounds, and choosing a gt
  theme.

- [Interactive
  charts](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-interactive.md):

  Ten recipes for team logos on web charts: ggiraph tooltips, hover
  highlights and logo axes, plotly logos as layout images, a reactable
  table, self-contained HTML and static PNG exports for social posts.

- [Surfaces and shot
  charts](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-surfaces-shot-charts.md):

  Ten recipes for playing surfaces: every sport’s surface, team colors
  and a center logo, NBA and WNBA shot charts with sdv_court_coords(), a
  rotated half court, a college shot chart, a hockey goal map, a
  baseball field and a football drive chart.

- [reactable
  Integration](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/reactable-integration.md):

### Recipes

Real-world tasks worked end to end: the brief, the data, a first draft,
the fixes that make it publishable, and the export at the size it will
be posted. Then static versions of SportsDataverse web visuals, and
end-to-end workflows.

- [NFL EPA
  Scatter](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nfl-epa-scatter.md):

  Make the offense-vs-defense EPA chart for social after the Super Bowl,
  from a first draft to 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080 exports.

- [CFB Conference
  Table](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-cfb-conference-table.md):

  Build the final Big Ten standings table for a newsletter, with logos,
  records and game-by-game margin bars, exported 1600 px wide and as a
  square for social.

- [NBA Net Rating
  Quadrant](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nba-net-rating-quadrant.md):

  Chart every NBA team’s offensive and defensive rating by conference,
  with logos, average lines and net-rating guides, for a blog post and a
  square social post.

- [WNBA Scoring Leaders
  Card](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-wnba-scoring-leaders-card.md):

  Make a WNBA scoring-leaders card with headshots, team logos and
  team-color bars, exported at 1080 x 1080 for Instagram and as a
  top-five 1200 x 675 cut for X.

- [MLB Run
  Differential](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-mlb-run-differential.md):

  Build a 1600 px blog graphic of MLB run differential: ranked bars in
  team colors with logos at the bar ends, beside the season’s running
  totals for the best and worst teams.

- [NHL Shot
  Map](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nhl-shot-map.md):

  Map every unblocked shot of the Stanley Cup clincher on a rink in team
  colors, sized by expected goals, with the goals, a scoreboard and
  exports at 1200 x 675 and 1600 x 900.

- [College Hoops
  Tiers](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-college-hoops-tiers.md):

  Turn a power rating into a Big Ten men’s basketball tier list with
  sdv_team_tiers(), the tiers cut at the rating gaps, exported for
  social at 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080.

- [Rank Bump
  Chart](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-rank-bump-chart.md):

  Draw the college football season’s AP Top 25 as a bump chart, with
  logos at the line ends and the biggest climbers called out, exported
  for a blog and for X.

- [Head-to-Head
  Card](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-head-to-head-card.md):

  Make a Super Bowl tale-of-the-tape card: two teams’ seasons as
  mirrored league-rank bars in team colors with dark-background logos,
  exported at 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080.

- [WBB Conference
  Standings](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-wbb-conference-standings.md):

  Build the final SEC women’s basketball standings table, with logos,
  every conference result as a win-loss strip and how each season ended,
  dressed in the champion’s colors and exported for a newsletter and a
  4:5 Instagram post.

### Leaderboards

Each league’s current season, rebuilt every week with this site: the
season to date while games are played, the last full season in the
offseason.

- [NFL Weekly
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-nfl-weekly.md):

  The NFL season to date, rebuilt every week: a power table by EPA per
  play, offense against defense, and a quarterback leaderboard with
  headshots.

- [College Football Weekly
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-cfb-weekly.md):

  The college football season to date, rebuilt every week: a top 25 by
  opponent-adjusted EPA per play, every FBS team by conference, and the
  top 25’s offense against its defense.

- [NBA
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-nba.md):

  The latest NBA season, rebuilt every week: standings with net rating,
  a net rating chart with logos on the axis, and the scoring leaders
  with headshots.

- [WNBA
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-wnba.md):

  The latest WNBA season, rebuilt every week: standings with net rating,
  offense against defense with logos, and the scoring leaders with
  headshots.

- [MLB
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-mlb.md):

  The latest MLB season, rebuilt every week: division standings, run
  differential with logos, and the league leaders with headshots.

- [NHL
  Leaderboard](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/leaderboard-nhl.md):

  The latest NHL season, rebuilt every week: standings, goal
  differential with logos on the axis, and the points leaders with
