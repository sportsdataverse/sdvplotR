### Per-Sport Articles

Data ingest with the companion SportsDataverse package, then sdvplotR
visualization, for each sport.

- [NFL Visualizations with nflverse and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/nfl-viz.md):

  Ten charts and tables from the 2025 NFL season with nflreadr and
  sdvplotR: offense against defense with logos, standings, quarterback
  headshots, relocated franchises and a team-colored field.

- [College Football Visualizations with cfbfastR and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cfb-viz.md):

  Ten charts and tables from the 2025 college football season with
  cfbfastR and sdvplotR: every FBS team on one scatter, conference
  standings, the Playoff bracket, quarterback headshots and tiers.

- [NBA Visualizations with hoopR and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/nba-viz.md):

  Ten charts and tables from the 2025-26 NBA season with hoopR and
  sdvplotR: a ratings quadrant, net-rating bars, a bump chart,
  headshots, a team-colored shot chart and an interactive version.

- [WNBA Visualizations with wehoop and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wnba-viz.md):

  Ten charts and tables from the 2026 WNBA season, the first with 15
  teams, with wehoop and sdvplotR: a ratings quadrant, expansion win
  races, headshots, a binned shot chart and an interactive chart.

- [MLB Visualizations with baseballr and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/mlb-viz.md):

  Ten worked examples on the 2026 MLB regular season: standings, run
  differential, Statcast, home run leaders, a spray chart, the division
  races, franchise moves and a tier list.

- [NHL Visualizations with fastRhockey and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/nhl-viz.md):

  Ten worked examples on the 2025-26 NHL season: standings, goal
  differential, expected goals, a shot map on the rink, scoring and
  goaltending leaders, the Coyotes-to-Utah lineage, the points race and
  the playoff tiers.

- [Men's College Basketball Visualizations with hoopR and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/mbb-viz.md):

  Division I men’s basketball has more than 360 teams in 31 conferences,
  and the conferences change from one season to the next.

- [Women's College Basketball Visualizations with wehoop and
  sdvplotR](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wbb-viz.md):

  Division I women’s basketball has more than 360 teams, and each shares
  its ESPN id with the school’s men’s team.

- [Soccer: any provider's shots on one
  pitch](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/soccer-viz.md):

  Convert soccer event coordinates from any major provider to one 105 x
  68 m pitch frame with sdv_pitch_coords(), then draw them with
  sdvplotR.

### Cookbooks

Task-oriented, cross-league recipes: logos and headshots in ggplot2,
team colors and themes, interactive charts, playing surfaces and shot
charts, and reactable tables (with reactablefmtr).

- [Logos, wordmarks and headshots in
  ggplot2](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-ggplot-logos.md):

  Twelve ggplot2 recipes: logos as points, at bar and line ends, on axes
  and beside titles, in tier lists, plus sizing, overlap, past seasons’
  marks, any image, headshots and social-size exports.

- [Team colors and
  themes](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-colors-themes.md):

  Ten recipes for team colors: a league’s palette, colors per row, team
  fill and outline scales, clashing colors, readable text on team fills,
  named palettes, conference colors, dark backgrounds, and choosing a gt
  theme.

- [Interactive
  charts](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-interactive.md):

  Twelve recipes for team logos on web charts: ggiraph tooltips, hover
  highlights and logo axes, plotly logos as layout images, leaflet logo
  markers, a reactable table, self-contained HTML and static PNG exports
  for social posts.

- [Surfaces and shot
  charts](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-surfaces-shot-charts.md):

  Eleven recipes for playing surfaces: every sport’s surface, team
  colors and a center logo, NBA and WNBA shot charts with
  sdv_court_coords(), a rotated half court, a college shot chart, a
  hockey goal map, a baseball field and a football drive chart and
  Euroleague shots on the FIBA court.

- [reactable
  Integration](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/reactable-integration.md):

  sdvplotR provides a family of helpers for embedding team logos,
  wordmarks, player headshots, and team-colored bars in reactable
  tables.

- [reactable and reactablefmtr
  cookbook](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-reactable.md):

  Ten recipes for team tables on the web: reactablefmtr data bars,
  heatmap tiles, icon sets, pill buttons, merged columns, image
  comparisons and themes, plus wordmarks, grouped rows, conference logos
  and expandable rows in reactable, across six leagues.

### Tables cookbook

gt tables on SportsDataverse data: the house themes, standings with cut
lines, percentile bars, rank changes, schedule matrices, tier lists,
faceted and snaked layouts, and saving for social. The gt\_\* toolkit
comes from Andrew Weatherman’s gtUtils.

- [SportsDataverse Table
  Themes](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/sdv-table-themes.md):

  The SportsDataverse table themes for gt: gt_theme_sdv() in light or
  dark, and gt_theme_sdv_team() dressed in one team’s colors.

- [Team Tables with the gt
  Toolkit](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/gt-toolkit.md):

  The two kinds of gt helpers on one table: the team layer of logos,
  headshots and colors, and the table toolkit of themes, legends and cut
  lines.

- [Styling Headers, Legends, and
  Captions](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/styling.md):

  Style headers, legends and captions in gt tables through the
  named-list arguments of gt_title_header() and the other helpers that
  take them.

- [Saving and Posting
  Tables](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/saving_tables.md):

  Save gt tables as images sized for each platform, crop the whitespace,
  and post the same table to the web and to social feeds.

- [Border
  Bars](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/border_bars.md):

  Colored bars above or below a gt table with gt_border_bars_top() and
  gt_border_bars_bottom(), stacked or as a single accent.

- [Percentile Bars and Cut
  Lines](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/delay_tables.md):

  Percentile bars, cut lines and significance marks that show which
  values in a gt table stand out and where one group ends and the next
  begins.

- [Creating Faceted
  Tables](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/grid_tables.md):

  Split a long gt table into side-by-side panels with gt_grid(), so 25
  rows fit an image shaped for posting.

- [Building a Schedule
  Matrix](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/schedule_matrix.md):

  A schedule matrix puts one team per row and one week per column, and
  draws each opponent’s logo in the cell where they met.

- [Creating Tier
  Lists](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/tier_list.md):

  A tier list ranks teams into colored bands, best band on top, each
  band a row of logos.

- [Winningest Franchises in Rolling
  Windows](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/window_wins.md):

  The NFL’s five winningest franchises in every ten-year window from
  1999-2008 to 2016-2025, each drawn as its logo and shaded by its Super
  Bowl wins.

### Recipes

Real-world tasks worked end to end: the brief, the data, a first draft,
the fixes that make it publishable, and the export at the size it will
be posted. Then static versions of SportsDataverse web visuals, and
end-to-end workflows.

- [NFL EPA
  Scatter](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nfl-epa-scatter.md):

  Make the offense-vs-defense EPA chart for social after the Super Bowl,
  from a first draft to 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080 exports.

- [CFB Conference
  Table](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-cfb-conference-table.md):

  Build the final Big Ten standings table for a newsletter, with logos,
  records and game-by-game margin bars, exported 1600 px wide and as a
  square for social.

- [NBA Net Rating
  Quadrant](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nba-net-rating-quadrant.md):

  Chart every NBA team’s offensive and defensive rating by conference,
  with logos, average lines and net-rating guides, for a blog post and a
  square social post.

- [WNBA Scoring Leaders
  Card](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-wnba-scoring-leaders-card.md):

  Make a WNBA scoring-leaders card with headshots, team logos and
  team-color bars, exported at 1080 x 1080 for Instagram and as a
  top-five 1200 x 675 cut for X.

- [MLB Run
  Differential](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-mlb-run-differential.md):

  Build a 1600 px blog graphic of MLB run differential: ranked bars in
  team colors with logos at the bar ends, beside the season’s running
  totals for the best and worst teams.

- [NHL Shot
  Map](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nhl-shot-map.md):

  Map every unblocked shot of the Stanley Cup clincher on a rink in team
  colors, sized by expected goals, with the goals, a scoreboard and
  exports at 1200 x 675 and 1600 x 900.

- [College Hoops
  Tiers](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-college-hoops-tiers.md):

  Turn a power rating into a Big Ten men’s basketball tier list with
  sdv_team_tiers(), the tiers cut at the rating gaps, exported for
  social at 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080.

- [Rank Bump
  Chart](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-rank-bump-chart.md):

  Draw the college football season’s AP Top 25 as a bump chart, with
  logos at the line ends and the biggest climbers called out, exported
  for a blog and for X.

- [Head-to-Head
  Card](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-head-to-head-card.md):

  Make a Super Bowl tale-of-the-tape card: two teams’ seasons as
  mirrored league-rank bars in team colors with dark-background logos,
  exported at 1200 x 675 and 1080 x 1080.

- [WBB Conference
  Standings](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-wbb-conference-standings.md):

  Build the final SEC women’s basketball standings table, with logos,
  every conference result as a win-loss strip and how each season ended,
  dressed in the champion’s colors and exported for a newsletter and a
  4:5 Instagram post.

- [Neighbour
  Ranks](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/neighbour-ranks.md):

  One team in the middle of an 11-row table, with the five teams ranked
  just above and below it, so the gaps to its neighbours show next to
  the rank.

- [Shot
  Grid](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/shot-grid.md):

  A shot chart cut into a grid of zones, each colored by a shooter’s
  percentage against the league from that spot, rather than one dot per
  attempt.

- [Rolling
  Form](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/rolling-form.md):

  Each team’s last 150 offensive plays against the 150 before them,
  drawn so a change in form shows before the season average catches up.

- [Signature
  Ribbon](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/signature-ribbon.md):

  A shooter’s profile as a ribbon: how often each distance is attempted
  and how well it is made, drawn along the shot distance.

- [End-to-End Workflows with sdvplotR and the SportsDataverse
  Ecosystem](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/workflows.md):

  End-to-end workflows: load data from SportsDataverse packages, draw it
  with sdvplotR, and export the charts and tables.

### Interactive

The recipes as charts and maps a reader can hover, filter and zoom, with
ggiraph, plotly and leaflet. Start with the Interactive Charts cookbook;
every leaderboard and per-sport article also ends with an interactive
version.

- [Interactive NFL EPA
  Scatter](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nfl-epa-interactive.md):

  Turn the offense-vs-defense EPA chart into a web chart: hover a logo
  for the team’s numbers and ranks, fade the other 31, and share it as
  one HTML file.

- [Interactive NBA Net
  Rating](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nba-net-rating-interactive.md):

  Build the NBA offense-vs-defense quadrant in plotly with team logos
  and hover details, then a net-rating bar chart with logos on the axis,
  and share both as web pages.

- [Interactive NHL Shot
  Map](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-nhl-shot-map-interactive.md):

  Turn the Stanley Cup clincher’s shot map into a web chart: hover a
  shot for the shooter, period, time, shot type and result, hover a
  shooter to light up all of his shots, and keep each team’s logo and
  score beside its end.

- [Interactive MLB Run
  Differential](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-mlb-run-differential-interactive.md):

  Make the season’s run differential ranking a web chart: team-color
  bars with logos on the axis, a hover card with each team’s record and
  runs, and a linked view of run differential against winning percentage
  with the Pythagorean expectation.

- [CFB Stadium
  Map](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/recipe-cfb-stadium-map.md):

  Put every FBS program on a map as its logo at its home stadium: hover
  for the school, click for the stadium, switch conferences on and off,
  and share it as one HTML file.

- [Team maps with
  leaflet](https://sdvplotR.sportsdataverse.org/articles/cookbook-leaflet-maps.md):

  Five leaflet maps with team logos as markers: every MLB ballpark with
  its dimensions, the parks by division, team-colored circles sized by
  wins, spring training with clustered markers, and the coast-to-coast
  Big Ten.
